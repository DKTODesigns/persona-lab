# PersonaLab

**AI-powered UX Research Intelligence Platform**

PersonaLab helps UX researchers accelerate discovery by transforming research briefs into evidence-based AI personas, conducting synthetic user interviews, and generating actionable research insights.

> ⚠️ AI-generated personas and interviews are research hypotheses, not replacements for real user research. All outputs should be validated with actual users.

---

## Overview

Traditional UX research requires significant time to recruit participants, synthesize findings, and identify patterns.

PersonaLab assists researchers by:

- Generating evidence-based personas from research inputs
- Clearly distinguishing evidence from assumptions
- Conducting AI-powered synthetic interviews
- Surfacing emerging insights during interviews
- Identifying unanswered research questions
- Supporting a structured research workflow

# Evidence-Based Personas

Every statement generated by PersonaLab is classified according to confidence:

- ✅ Evidence
- 🔵 Inference
- 🟠 Assumption
- ⚪ Unknown

Researchers can immediately distinguish verified findings from hypotheses, making it easier to identify areas requiring further validation.

# AI Interview Simulator

Conduct moderated interviews with AI-generated personas.

Each persona responds consistently based on its:

- Goals
- Frustrations
- Behaviors
- Context
- Accessibility needs
- Evidence confidence levels

Rather than acting like a generic chatbot, the Interview Simulator behaves like a research participant whose responses remain grounded in the available evidence.

# Why PersonaLab?

Traditional UX research often involves weeks of recruiting participants, synthesizing notes, identifying patterns, and documenting findings.

PersonaLab helps researchers:

- Explore ideas earlier
- Test assumptions faster
- Generate richer interview guides
- Surface research gaps sooner
- Spend more time validating with real users

It is designed to accelerate—not replace—the UX research process.

---

## Philosophy

Good research is evidence-driven.

PersonaLab encourages researchers to distinguish between:

- What is known
- What is inferred
- What is assumed
- What still needs validation

By making confidence visible throughout the workflow, PersonaLab helps teams make better product decisions while keeping real user research at the center of the process.
